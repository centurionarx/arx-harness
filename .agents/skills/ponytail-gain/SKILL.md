# Ponytail Gain

Report the published agentic benchmark, not a local measurement: 12 feature tasks in full-stack-fastapi-template, Haiku 4.5, n=4 per arm. Upstream reports mean reductions of 54% in lines of code, 22% in tokens, 20% in cost, and 27% in time against its no-skill baseline. These are workload-specific results, not guarantees for arx or other models.

Source: https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail/blob/c982cd411abb53323c4baa1baa3c2f020b8d0b08/benchmarks/results/2026-06-18-agentic.md
